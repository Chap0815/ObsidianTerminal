# Community conduct

Participation in this project's issues, discussions and reviews should be
respectful, constructive and relevant.

- Critique code and evidence, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate or publish personal information.
- Do not post scams, referral spam, impersonation or profit guarantees.
- Do not pressure others to disclose credentials, transfer funds or enable LIVE.
- Respect the project license and the privacy of financial information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock a discussion or restrict
participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct
and available context.

Use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for abusive content. Do not post sensitive
personal details publicly when reporting an incident. Security vulnerabilities
are handled separately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
